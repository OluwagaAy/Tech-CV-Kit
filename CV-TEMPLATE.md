## ❌ Common mistakes I see every week

- A photo, date of birth, state of origin and religion on the CV. Leave them off unless the ad asks.
- "Objective: to work in a reputable organisation..." Every CV says this. Write a real profile instead.
- Listing every Microsoft Office app as a skill.
- Duties instead of results.
- A CV named `CV final final 2.docx`.
