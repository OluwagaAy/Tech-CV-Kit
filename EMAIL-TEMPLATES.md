# ✉️ Email Templates

Copy, fill in the brackets, and send. Keep every email **short**: recruiters read on their phones.

---

## 1. Applying by email

**Subject:** use the **exact** subject line from the job ad. If none is given: `Application – [Job Title] – [Your Name]`

```
Dear Hiring Manager,

I'm applying for the [Job Title] role advertised on [where you saw it]. I have [1 line: your most relevant skill, tool or certification], and I recently [1 line: a project or result that matches the role].

My CV is attached. I'd welcome the chance to discuss how I can support your team.

Kind regards,
[Full Name]
[Phone] · [LinkedIn URL]
```

✅ Attach your CV as PDF: `Firstname-Lastname-CV.pdf`
✅ Send from a professional email address (firstname.lastname@...)
❌ Don't write "Please find attached my CV for your perusal"

---

## 2. Following up (5 to 7 working days later)

**Subject:** `Re: Application – [Job Title] – [Your Name]`

```
Dear [Name / Hiring Manager],

I'm following up on my application for the [Job Title] role, sent on [date]. I'm still very interested and happy to share anything else you need.

Thank you for your time.

Kind regards,
[Full Name]
```

Follow up **once**. If there's no reply, move on and keep applying.

---

## 3. Thank you after an interview (same day)

**Subject:** `Thank you – [Job Title] interview`

```
Dear [Interviewer's Name],

Thank you for speaking with me today about the [Job Title] role. I enjoyed learning about [something specific they mentioned], and it made me even more excited about the opportunity.

[Optional: one line answering a question better than you did in the interview.]

I look forward to hearing from you.

Kind regards,
[Full Name]
```

---

## 4. LinkedIn message to a recruiter

Keep it under 300 characters so it fits in a connection note.

```
Hi [Name], I'm a [your role/level] with [key skill or cert], based in [city]. I saw you recruit for [type of roles] and would love to be considered for anything suitable. Thank you!
```
